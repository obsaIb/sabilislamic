Sabil Islamic Organization

is an independent, non-governmental, non-political, and non-profit organization dedicated to serving communities through faith, compassion, and charitable service.

About Us

Sabil Islamic Organization is officially registered with the Ethiopian Islamic Affairs Supreme Council under Registration No. 200/269/18 on 20 July 2026.

Inspired by the timeless values of Islam, we strive to improve lives, strengthen communities, and promote hope by implementing projects that create lasting social and spiritual impact.

Vision

To build compassionate, educated, and self-reliant communities inspired by the values and teachings of Islam.

Mission

To serve humanity by implementing sustainable humanitarian, educational, and religious initiatives that uplift lives, strengthen communities, and earn the pleasure of Allah through sincerity, integrity, and excellence.

Our Programs

Our activities include, but are not limited to:

01

Mosque Construction and Development

Building and renovating mosques that serve as centers of worship, education, and community development.

02

Islamic Education

Establishing and supporting madrasas, educational centers, and programs that help children and adults learn the Qur’an and authentic Islamic teachings.

03

Qur’an Distribution

Providing copies of the Holy Qur’an and beneficial Islamic literature to individuals, schools, mosques, and underserved communities.

04

Water Projects

Constructing water wells and providing access to clean and safe drinking water where it is most needed.

05

⁠Zakat Distribution

Collecting and distributing Zakat in accordance with Islamic principles to eligible recipients, helping alleviate poverty and support those in need.

06

Sacrificial Programs

Organizing and facilitating the slaughter and distribution of Qurban, Aqiqah, and Nadhr (vow) sacrifices, ensuring that the meat reaches needy families.

Your Support Changes Lives

With Allah's permission, every donation, partnership, and volunteer effort brings hope to those in need. We welcome organizations and Ahlul Khayr to join us in this blessed mission.
